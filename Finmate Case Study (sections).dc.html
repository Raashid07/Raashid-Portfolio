03 — The problem

Money apps that make money feel harder.

Managing personal finances often means switching between dense dashboards, unfamiliar terms and numbers without context. Instead of feeling in control, people feel overwhelmed — and avoid looking at their money altogether.

Too much complexity Hard-to-read information No clear next step
04 — The goal

Create a simple, understandable and visually organized finance experience that helps users make better decisions.
